Torii Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Information stored on your device

Goshuin records, visit notes, omikuji history, selected language, and saved photos are stored locally on your device. You can delete this data from the app settings or by uninstalling the app.

Omikuji image interpretation

When you request an interpretation, the selected image is resized and sent securely to our AI gateway. The image is used only to produce the requested result and is not retained by our server after processing. The interpretation is cultural guidance and does not guarantee future events.

Subscriptions and abuse prevention

RevenueCat, Apple App Store, and Google Play process subscription status and purchases under their respective privacy policies. We use a randomly generated app installation identifier, transformed before server-side storage, to enforce the free-use and daily rate limits. We do not use advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Torii Guide プライバシーポリシー

御朱印・参拝メモ・おみくじ履歴・保存画像・言語設定は端末内に保存されます。おみくじ判定を依頼した場合のみ、縮小した画像を暗号化通信でAI判定ゲートウェイへ送信し、処理後にサーバーへ保存しません。購入状態の管理にはRevenueCat、App Store、Google Playを利用します。無料回数・日次上限の不正利用防止には、ランダムに生成したアプリ設置IDを変換して使用します。広告SDKおよびアプリ横断トラッキングは使用しません。
